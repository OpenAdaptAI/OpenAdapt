Agent-to-agent handoffs: `send` attaches a run's receipt to what one agent hands another (inline, detached beside a file, or POSTed to a webhook); `verify` checks one on arrival against your pinned keys (who signed, what was checked, and that a valid signature proves who signed, not that the claim is true); `key` and `trust` exchange receipt keys between parties; `list` shows handoffs to other parties and what their systems confirmed.
