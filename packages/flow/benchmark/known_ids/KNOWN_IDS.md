# Known-ID cross-check: dense record list, before and after

Generated by `python -m openadapt_flow.validation.known_ids_dense`. Synthetic data only.

## What was measured

- Surface: the dense record list of `benchmark/dense_surface` (40 rows, seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5), rendered in a browser and read by RapidOCR, with the replayer's band extraction and its 2x retry.
- Replay conditions: hi_res_arial, native_arial, small_dense, serif_drift. Click configs: click_name, click_action.
- No list: the shipped OCR verdict.
- List + same-frame reads (earlier design): the native read and the 2x upscale of the same frame passed through `runtime.known_ids.resolve_lookalike_abstain`.
- List + fresh capture (current design): the native read of the first frame and a native read of a new screenshot of the same screen, as `Replayer._known_id_lift` does.
- In both list columns every identifier in the seed's table is the complete known-ID list.
- Denominator for false stops: armed clicks on the correct row. Denominator for false accepts: armed clicks on the adjacent sibling row (same count).

## How to read this

In the `id_*` classes the sibling's identifier differs from the target's only by a look-alike glyph, and both are in the list. OCR cannot tell them apart, so the run still stops; that stop is correct, not a false stop in any useful sense. It is counted here so the denominator stays the full published one.

The same-frame design accepted a lift only when the native read and the 2x upscale both abstained on a look-alike identifier. The fresh capture design needs the native read of two separate screenshots to agree instead; a screen that changes between captures blocks the lift. On this fixture the two designs differ by 1 of 480 correct-row clicks. Because the fixture's fresh captures are pixel-identical (see Limits), that difference comes only from clicks where the 2x upscale read the band differently from the native read.

The remaining stops are band mismatches that have nothing to do with look-alike glyphs (dense-row OCR drops or garbles a name cell).

## Limits

- The lift is only as safe as the list is complete. The list must come from a configured export of the system of record, and an operator confirms its source and completeness at admission; replay halts if that confirmation is missing, the export changed, or it is older than the configured age.
- One fixture, one OCR engine (RapidOCR), synthetic rows. A different renderer, font or OCR engine moves these numbers.
- The fixture renders deterministically: 20 of 20 fresh renders (one per seed and replay condition) were pixel-identical to the first. On those, the second read repeats the first. What the fresh capture guards against (a caret, a partial repaint or a screen that changed between captures) does not occur in this fixture, so these numbers measure the availability cost of the design, not its protection.
