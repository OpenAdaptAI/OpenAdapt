The verdict gives the signer role from the receiver's registry, integrity, checks passed and not checked, and "confirmed in the other party's system" lines; it always says a valid signature proves who signed, not that the claim is true, binds the payload as the receipt's `payload` subject (re-signed by the run's own key; other bytes or no payload subject are refused), and counts a confirmation as independent only when it was read from the other party's system.
